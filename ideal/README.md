# Ideal

Run your Ideal from Claude, and grow it. Ideal (getonideal.com) is where creators publish video, music and posts,
sell on the Market, place their work on each other's pages and have their own AI answering visitors.

## Use it

Connect the Ideal connector from this plugin's Connectors tab and sign in (or create your creator account). Then ask
Claude to set you up on Ideal, to review your week, or to publish something today. Claude checks what is done and
what is missing, writes your profile, posts and listings for you to approve, finds videos and articles in your files
and uploads them, and answers placement requests and messages — always showing you first.

## Data

The plugin talks to your Ideal account through mcp.getonideal.com, as you, after you sign in. It never spends your
IDL, cashes out or changes your password. You can see everything it did, and disconnect, at
getonideal.com/create/claude.
